Privacy notice (version 2026-11-23)

  • What we keep: your name, e-mail, mobile number, role, sales and commissions, and your payout details (UPI id or bank account, PAN), stored encrypted.
  • Why: to run your account, attribute sales, pay you, and meet tax rules.
  • Who sees it: your name and e-mail go to Moneypenny and Corporate Monopoly so you have one login. Payout details go to Razorpay / RazorpayX when you are paid. Customers' e-mails are kept only as a one-way code.

  • Erasure limits: the owner completes erasure after money owed and any open payout are settled. Moneypenny and Corporate Monopoly keep their own copy; erasure there goes through those products.
  • How long we keep things: click records for 180 days and prospect records for 365 days, then they are removed. Sales, commission and action-log records use only internal ids and are kept after erasure so that the accounts stay correct; your name, e-mail, mobile number and payout details are removed.
