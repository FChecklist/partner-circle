Privacy notice (version 2026-11-23)

  • What we keep: the name, e-mail, mobile number, role, sales and commissions, and the payout details (UPI id or bank account, PAN), stored encrypted.
  • Why: to run the account, attribute sales, pay the partner, and meet tax rules.
  • Who sees it: the name and e-mail go to Moneypenny and Corporate Monopoly so there is one login. Payout details go to Razorpay when you are paid. Customers' e-mails are kept only as a one-way code.
  • Choices: in the account, under "Data and privacy", a partner can download what we hold, rotate the key, or ask for erasure.
  • Erasure limits: the owner completes erasure after money owed and any open payout are settled. Moneypenny and Corporate Monopoly keep their own copy; erasure there goes through those products.
  • How long we keep things: click records for 180 days and prospect records for 365 days, then they are removed. Sales, commission and action-log records use only internal ids and are kept after erasure so that the accounts stay correct; the name, e-mail, mobile number and payout details are removed.
  • Embed snippet: on our own sites it stores the partner click id in a first-party cookie and local storage for 30 days.
